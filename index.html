About Happy Cake

From a family kitchen to your celebrations.

Happy Cake began with a simple idea — bake cakes the way you would at home, for the people you love. Our guests kept saying “it tastes just like I made it myself.” With roots in Kazakhstan and a home in Sugar Land, every cake is handmade and hand-packed from recipes perfected over years.
